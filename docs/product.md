# 🇮🇳 SIH26043 Product Vision & Problem Statement

## Problem Statement
Bridging the disconnect between citizen-reported infrastructural and civic grievances, and mobilizing academic talent and industrial CSR funding to create scalable, indigenous solutions.

## Core Value Pillars
1. **Citizen Empowerment**: Voice, photo, and geolocation reporting with end-to-end transparency.
2. **AI Triage & Deduplication**: Eliminates manual filtering backlogs for government bodies.
3. **Academic R&D Mobilization**: Connects universities directly to real national problems.
4. **Corporate CSR Synergy**: Transparent milestone-based fund deployment with compliance audit trails.
